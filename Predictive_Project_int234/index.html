DATA & METHODOLOGY

What was calculated.

The same preparation and modeling steps used in the original Python analysis, documented as part of the project.

01

Data cleaning

Duplicate rows were identified and removed. 12,205 unique sessions remained from the original 12,330.

02

Type conversion

Weekend and Revenue were converted to integer values for modeling.

03

Feature engineering

Added Total_Pages, Total_Duration, and Avg_Time_Per_Page.

04

Categorical encoding

Month and VisitorType were one-hot encoded with the first category dropped.

05

Train/test split

80% training and 20% testing with random_state=42 and stratification by Revenue.

06

Scaling

StandardScaler was fitted on the training data and then applied to the test data.
